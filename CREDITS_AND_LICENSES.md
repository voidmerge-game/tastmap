# The Unification of Italy, 1815–1871 — Credits & Licenses

Short historical map documentary. Estimated duration: **114.96 s** at 30 fps (3,449 frames). The final video has not been rendered.

## Narration and script
Original English-language narration script. American-English neural narration generated for this project; word-level timings produced with Whisper (faster-whisper `small.en`) and aligned to the audio.

## Maps and geographic data
- Natural Earth 10m Admin-1 States & Provinces, public domain. Modern administrative units were unioned and clipped to form approximate 1815 and 1871 political boundaries. These are illustrative reconstructions, not a historical GIS boundary dataset.
- NASA Blue Marble, July 2004, public domain.
- Natural Earth GRAY_HR_SR_OB relief raster, public domain.
- NASA global cloud composite, public domain.

## Sound effects and artwork
Sound effects were synthesized procedurally for this production with `scripts/make_sfx.py`. The operations-table background was generated specifically for this film. Flags and map annotations are original vector artwork drawn in code.
